# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This is a collaborative school project. Every member deserves respectful participation, accurate credit, access to relevant project information, and a fair opportunity to contribute and present. Repository administration and task assignments do not make one member the owner of the team's work.

These expectations apply to project code reviews, issues, chats, meetings, presentations, peer feedback, and communications made on behalf of the team.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work respectfully and explain technical criticism with evidence. Make room for questions and different experience levels.
- Keep agreed responsibilities visible, communicate blockers early, and offer help without taking credit for another person's work.
- Record contributions and assessment evidence honestly. Disclose permitted AI assistance and reused work according to instructor rules.
- Protect classmates' contact details, private communications, credentials, and other sensitive information. Ask before sharing someone's material beyond its agreed audience.
- Respect accessibility needs, reasonable boundaries, and the right to decline an uncomfortable interaction.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, unwanted sexual attention, retaliation, targeted humiliation, publishing private information, and deliberate sabotage are unacceptable. So are knowingly falsifying contribution records or presenting collective work as exclusively personal work. Technical disagreement alone is not misconduct.

## Raising a concern

Use an existing private communication channel to contact an uninvolved team member you trust, or contact the course instructor directly. You do not have to report through the repository account holder or a person involved in the concern. Avoid public issues for sensitive reports. Share only the information needed to understand what happened and what support or action you seek; do not forward secrets or unrelated private messages.

## Handling concerns

Anyone directly involved in an allegation should not decide its outcome. Uninvolved members should listen to those affected, keep disclosure limited to people needed to address the concern, and seek instructor help when safety, assessment, a conflict of interest, or an unresolved dispute is involved. Confidentiality cannot be promised where school procedures require reporting.

Responses should fit the circumstances: clarify expectations, correct attribution, agree on communication boundaries, or request instructor mediation. Document decisions privately and allow concerns about the handling to be raised with the instructor. Only authorized school personnel decide grades or formal disciplinary outcomes. No member may use project access or a complaint to retaliate or claim sole ownership of the repository.
